# MAVROSPY

June 2024 - May 2025

**[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/bandofpv/mavrospy)**

## Story

[MAVROS](http://wiki.ros.org/mavros) is a [MAVLink](https://mavlink.io/en/) extendable communication node for ROS1 and ROS2. In other words, it allows users to integrate their ROS based architectures with [Ardupilot](https://ardupilot.org/ardupilot/) and/or [PX4](https://docs.px4.io/main/en/) autopliot firmwares which support MAVLink communication. By incorperating ROS and multicopter autopilots, users can easily dive into more advanced autonomous navigation systems like motion capture, lidar, obstacle avoidance, visual inertial odometry (VIO), and more.

This can be very useful as it acts as a abstraction layer between your ROS topics and your flight controller. You can simply send ROS topics through MAVROS and let it translate those topics to MAVLink messages which the flight controller can understand.

MAVROSPY serves as an additional layer to the MAVROS node. It allows users to easily manipulate the movement of their multicopter with simple Python methods while still integrating with the ROS framework. As such, it makes it even easier to tackle on your advanced ROS projects.

## Hardware Support

MAVROSPY is supported on a PX4 (v1.15.4) flight controller with a Jetson Orin Nano (JetPack 6.2), Jetson Nano (JetPack 4.6), or a RPi4 (Ubuntu 22.04 MATE) companion computer. Additionally, it supported on ROS2 Humble and ROS1 Noetic.
